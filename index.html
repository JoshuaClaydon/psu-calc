Why headroom matters

PSUs run most efficiently at 50–80% of rated capacity. The 80 PLUS certification tests exactly this — peak efficiency is rarely at full load.

Running above 90% load for extended periods increases heat, shortens component lifespan, and leaves no buffer for GPU power spikes during boost. A 20% margin keeps you in the safe zone.

Planning to overclock, add drives, or upgrade your GPU in the next year? Sizing up one tier now is cheaper than buying a second PSU.

Choosing a PSU

  • Buy from reputable brands: Seasonic, Corsair, be quiet!, Fractal
  • 80+ Gold or higher for any daily-use build
  • Fully modular cables help with airflow
  • RTX 40/50 series needs 16-pin or ATX 3.0 PSU
  • Check PSU length fits your case (SFX vs ATX)

About

TDP figures reflect manufacturers' peak power draw (Intel PL2, AMD TDP, Nvidia TGP). Real-world draw varies ±10–15%.
